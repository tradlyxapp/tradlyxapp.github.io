Private apps

Tradlyx

What this is

Tradlyx is the home of a few small private apps, built and run by one person for personal and household use. They run on a computer at home, and none of them is offered to the public: each has its own sign-in, for the few people who use it.

The apps

Tradlyx

stocks.tradlyx.app · one user, the owner

A personal market analyst. It uses Claude, an AI model made by Anthropic, to read market news, company filings, earnings calls and public chatter, and turns them into clear calls (buy, sell or hold) with their reasons. It keeps a practice portfolio, grades its own record in the open, and can place orders in the owner's own brokerage account within limits the owner sets, or leave every order for the owner to approve.

Tradlyx's calls are analysis for its owner, not financial advice, and investing can lose money.

Household

household.tradlyx.app · the members of one household

A private app for one household: a shared to-do list with a morning email, email replies that update the list, the members' Google Tasks kept in sync, grocery and shopping lists, a shared calendar, the household library, gym plans, the day's top news, and the home itself (lights, speakers, the thermostat and the photos on the Frame TV). Google's sign-in screen shows it as "Household Reminders".

The apps keep their data on that computer at home, show no ads, and never sell or share it. The privacy policy lists exactly what each app keeps and which services it uses.
